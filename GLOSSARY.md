# Bookshelf reader

The Bookshelf reader extends Jellyfin’s reader for comics, PDFs, and EPUBs.

## Language

**Reading order**:
The sequence of content from the start to the end of a book. First and Last identify its endpoints for both left-to-right and right-to-left books.

**Vertical mode**:
A reading mode in which the book is laid out continuously and the reader moves through it by scrolling up or down.
